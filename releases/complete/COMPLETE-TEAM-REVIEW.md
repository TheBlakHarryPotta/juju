## Marcus's assessment

The package now connects actual work instead of leaving three separate demos: a full conversation can produce reviewed sales drafts and a next step; approved requests can be compared with a dated forecast; completed groups can produce a source-backed closeout and a future rebooking cycle. The existing weekly report uses the same underlying records and distinguishes preparation/planning from completed contacts.

The most important limits are visible in the app. Match quality depends on current, correctly scoped forecast and request data. Actualized closeouts require actual production. Contracts remain drafts until their normal review/signing workflow. The tools do not log into Hilton systems, hold inventory or send outreach automatically. Real source reports and Safari/Mac output still need the user's final source check.

All three initial ideas are delivered. Further optional work would be live employer-approved integrations, background notifications, richer market research and source-backed tour brief/conversion dashboards. Those are separate future additions, not hidden dependencies for the implemented local workflows.
