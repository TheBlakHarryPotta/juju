# Linked save and undo review

The shared transaction service prepares a cold clone of the active hotel's state. The callback may change only the sales workflow collections, opportunities, contracts, activities, planned tasks, repeat-business data and matcher data. It cannot change hotel settings, report dates, signatures or its own receipt ledger. Callbacks must be synchronous; all asynchronous source preparation happens before the business-state commit.

The storage queue flushes earlier pending captures, then checks the expected live state, property and database revision. One IndexedDB snapshot transaction stores the entire accepted business change and its receipt. State is published to the UI only after the durable transaction succeeds, and only to the original active hotel. While opening a hotel, edits and linked commits wait for its pending snapshot load to finish, so a delayed load cannot erase a newer package. While saving, the interface is inert, legacy save timers are cleared, restore and new conflicting commits are blocked. A failed durable write leaves the live business collections and receipt ledger unchanged. Source originals retained before a failed commit may remain as evidence; they are separate from the atomic business update.

Receipts hold record-level before/after patches. Records in ID-bearing arrays are compared by identity rather than position. Undo checks every affected record before reversing anything; it preserves unrelated additions/edits and rejects changed affected records. It also rejects removal when later records refer to created IDs, including Repeat Business source links. It does not restore an old whole-hotel snapshot over later work. The undone receipt stays in the audit trail, and normal encrypted backups retain the trail.

Source review, full-suite regression and failure-injection evidence are recorded in INNOVATIONS-VALIDATION.md and tests/transactions.spec.js. The scope is local application consistency, not a cryptographically immutable ledger or a claim that anyone with full device/developer access cannot edit local data.
